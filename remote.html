01 About

I'm a software engineer based in Iran, working across the stack — Python on the backend, TypeScript and React on the front. Lately most of my energy goes into AI tooling: MCP servers, agent workflows, and the unglamorous plumbing that makes them reliable.

Alongside that I co-founded Virak Academy, where I mentor developers who are early in their careers. Teaching keeps me honest — you can't hand-wave an explanation to someone who's genuinely trying to understand.

I'm always on the lookout for new learning opportunities. If you're building something interesting, I'd like to hear about it.
