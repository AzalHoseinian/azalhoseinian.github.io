How I work

My background is software engineering — backends, interfaces, developer tooling. That's the ground I build on. What I build on it now is AI for healthcare.

The method is deliberately simple and repeatable: choose a real problem, build something against it, publish the code, and document the result — including the attempts that don't work. Then do it again.

Every loop leaves a trace on GitHub. That trace, not a job title, is the portfolio I'm building.
